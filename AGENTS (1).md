# AGENTS.md

# PROJECT: AI MARKET INTELLIGENCE APP

## PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Build a premium AI-powered market intelligence mobile application.

This application is NOT a signal-selling service.

This application is NOT a prediction machine.

This application is a decision-support platform that helps traders understand:

- What matters
- Why it matters
- What changed
- Who is in control
- Where opportunities may exist
- How strong those opportunities are
- What risks exist

The application should simplify complex market information into clear actionable insights.

The goal is to become the most useful market intelligence app for retail traders.

The app must feel professional, modern, premium, fast, and trustworthy.

---

# APP DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

## Design Philosophy

The application should look premium.

Think:

- Clean
- Minimal
- Professional
- Modern
- Black Theme

Avoid:

- Clutter
- Bright colours everywhere
- Excessive indicators
- Information overload

The app should make users feel they are using a professional trading intelligence platform.

# PAGE 1: HOME

Purpose:

Show the most important market opportunities.

Home page should immediately answer:

- What should I pay attention to right now?
- What markets are active?
- Where are buyers and sellers strongest?
- What opportunities have the highest probability?

Display:

- Asset Name
- Bias
- Confidence Score
- Buyer Control Score
- Volume Score
- News Impact Score
- Reversal Risk Score
- Trade Quality Rating

# PAGE 2: NEWS

Purpose:

Explain what matters.

The News page is not a news feed.

The News page is a filtered intelligence feed.

The AI must determine:

- Which news matters
- Which news does not matter
- Why the news matters
- How important it is
- Which assets it affects

Every article receives:

- Importance Score (0-10)
- Relevance Score (0-100)
- Affected Assets
- Expected Impact Duration

Include plain-English explanations.

# REVERSAL DETECTION ENGINE

- Volume Exhaustion
- Market Structure Shift
- Failed Breakout
- Liquidity Sweep

# CONFIDENCE ENGINE

Confidence must be calculated from:

- News
- Macro Conditions
- Volume
- Market Structure
- Trend Strength
- Historical Similar Signals

Output 0-100%.

# EXPLANATION ENGINE

Every alert must answer:

- What happened?

- Why now?
- Who is in control?

- How confident is the system?
